What We Offer

Training Programs

Every dog is unique. We tailor our approach to your dog's personality, age, and your goals.

The Essentials Program

4-Week On-Leash Training

Designed for owners who want a well-mannered dog with reliable obedience foundations and better behavior at home and in public.

Program Focus:

  • Loose leash walking
  • Structured heel
  • Sit, down, place, and recall foundations
  • Impulse control
  • Calm household behavior
  • Engagement and focus
  • Public manners and social neutrality

Ideal For: Dogs who need structure, better manners, and reliable obedience on leash. 4 months old and up.

The Freedom Program

6-Week Off-Leash Training

Our most popular program for owners who want advanced obedience and true reliability around distractions.

Program Focus:

  • Advanced obedience
  • Reliable off-leash recall
  • Off-leash heel
  • Extended place command
  • High distraction training
  • Public access training
  • Advanced impulse control
  • Real-world reliability

Ideal For: Owners who want freedom, reliability, and the ability to confidently take their dog anywhere. 5 months old and up.

The Perfect Start Program

12-Week Raise & Train

A fully immersive puppy development program focused on raising a confident, well-socialized, obedient dog from the very beginning.

Program Focus:

  • Potty training foundations
  • Crate training
  • Socialization and confidence building
  • Marker training and engagement
  • Proper play and structure
  • Basic obedience
  • Leash obedience
  • Environmental exposure
  • Calmness and impulse control
  • Raising a stable, neutral puppy
  • Off-leash obedience

Ideal For: Busy owners who want professional guidance during the most important stages of puppy development. From 8 weeks to 4 months old.
